Modifications: the full 16-minute original (1 - 4 February 2026) was
sampled every 24th frame and retimed to a 40-second scrub track, so the
whole four-day span - including the five X-class flares - is reachable
by scrolling. Scaled 1024x1024 -> 900x900 and re-encoded H.264 CRF 28
with a 10-frame GOP so seeking lands cheaply. No frames were reordered
and nothing was recoloured; the imagery is NASA's, and their burned-in
timestamps are left intact.

NASA media is generally free to use with credit. The NASA insignia/logo
is not used here. Credit is displayed in the running interface, not only
in this file.
