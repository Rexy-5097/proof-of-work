Modifications: the full 16-minute original (1 - 4 February 2026) was
sampled every 24th frame and retimed to a 40-second scrub track, so the
whole four-day span - including the five X-class flares - is reachable
by scrolling. Scaled 1024x1024 -> 768x768, resampled to 15 fps and
re-encoded H.264 CRF 28 with a 1-second GOP. The delivered file is
3.6 MB against the original 301 MB; measured SSIM against the 900x900
30 fps version this replaces is 0.957, and at the size it is displayed
the two are indistinguishable. The smaller file is what makes the scrub
smooth on a slow connection - at 7.4 MB the star stepped in visible
jumps because the decoder was waiting on the network. No frames were
reordered and nothing was recoloured; the imagery is NASA's, and their
burned-in timestamps are left intact.

NASA media is generally free to use with credit. The NASA insignia/logo
is not used here. Credit is displayed in the running interface, not only
in this file.
